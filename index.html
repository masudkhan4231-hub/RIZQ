Questions

Is the honey really pure?

Yes. We sell it as collected, with no added sugar. Crystallised honey is natural; warm the jar in a bowl of warm water.

What is kachi ghani mustard oil?

Mustard seeds pressed cold, without heating. It keeps the strong smell and taste used in bhorta and pickles.

When is khejurer gur available?

Fresh date palm gur comes in winter. Out of season, tap “Notify me” and we will message you.

Can I return a product?

Message us on WhatsApp within 24 hours of delivery if something is wrong.
